    Quality tiers

    Tiers start from each project's status, what it's confirmed to sync and how it was tested. S is picked by hand. They're a guide: rate the mods you've played to help the next person.

    Co-op %

    How much of the game you can play together. It starts as an estimate from the project's status and from what's confirmed to sync between players: the story, enemies, the world, items and seeing each other. Once three or more players have voted on a mod, their average replaces the estimate.

    Played one? Open it, say how much of the game worked in co-op, rate it and say how your session went. No account needed, and one vote per mod per person (you can change yours).
